Direct In-Browser Architecture

Browser-Native Canvas & Video

Your video files stay entirely on your device. Video frames are decoded locally, rendered to an HTML5 Canvas, and stylized with CSS and scanline filters.

IndexedDB Project Persistence

Work without fear of lost edits. Timeline clips, text titles, volume, and playback speed automatically persist in your browser's IndexedDB database.

Load Raw Media

Select or drag & drop an MP4 or WebM video file. The file is loaded directly into the browser via Blob Object URLs—no upload waits or external servers.

Trim, Filter & Overlay

Split clips at the playhead, delete segments, and apply 4 retro filters: CRT Scanlines, VHS Cyber, Neon Glitch, and Retro Mono. Add title text overlays with custom timestamps and neon styling.

Export WebM & Project JSON

Render canvas frames directly in your browser using the MediaRecorder API to produce a downloadable WebM video file, or backup your project configuration as a portable JSON file.
